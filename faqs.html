FAQs

Learners and organisations

For learners

What is the Crea8it Lab?

The Lab is our learning app where cohorts work through programs, submit tasks and track progress. You can open it from the footer of any page (Enter Lab).

How do I join a program?

Open the Lab, choose the join tab, and fill in your details with the Organisation Code your cohort admin gave you.

Where do I get an Organisation Code?

From the person or organisation running your cohort. If you don't have one, ask them, or message us on WhatsApp.

Do I need a payment code?

Only if your program requires one. Leave it blank otherwise. Payment codes are issued by whoever runs your program.

Who can see my details?

Your cohort admin can see your name, email, WhatsApp number, progress and submissions. See our Privacy & Cookies page for the full list.

Do you use cookies?

Our website sets none of its own and has no analytics. The Lab uses only what it needs to keep you logged in.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email Abdulwrite77@gmail.com and we will act within 30 days.

Will I get a certificate?

Programs that offer one will issue it on completion. Certificates confirm completion; they are not accredited qualifications.

For organisations

Can I run my own cohort on the Lab?

Yes. See Run Your Own Cohort, then request early access and we will help you set up.

How much does it cost?

Fees are flexible and agreed per cohort. Tell us about your program and we will confirm a fee in writing.

What do I get?

Your own organisation space, a code to share with participants, and tools to manage participants and review submissions.

Can I charge my own participants?

Yes. You set the price and collect it yourself, including through payment codes you issue. We do not take a share unless we agree so in writing.

How is my participants' data handled?

You may use it only to run your cohort. The details are in the Organisation Terms.

What happens if I stop?

You can stop any time. You then have 30 days to export your data before it is deleted.
